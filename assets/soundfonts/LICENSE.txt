florestan-subset.sf2
====================

Origin URL : https://raw.githubusercontent.com/schellingb/TinySoundFont/main/examples/florestan-subset.sf2
             (file "examples/florestan-subset.sf2" in the TinySoundFont repository,
              https://github.com/schellingb/TinySoundFont, where it ships as the
              example SoundFont; the library itself is MIT-licensed)
Author     : Nando Florestan (http://nandoflorestan.cjb.net, http://go.to/florestan)
             — subset of the "Florestan" SoundFont instrument collection
License    : Public Domain

The SoundFont's own INFO chunk declares:
    ICOP = "Public Domain"
    ICMT = "http://go.to/florestan  http://nandoflorestan.cjb.net"
The author's Florestan banks are also listed as "Public domain" on the
Polyphone SoundFont library (https://www.polyphone.io/en/soundfonts/pianos/281-000-florestan-piano).

No rights reserved. The file may be used, copied, modified and redistributed
for any purpose without restriction or attribution requirement.

Contents (SF2 format, 17 presets, all bank 0, General MIDI program numbers):
  2 Piano, 10 Music Box, 12 Marimba, 19 Church Org.1, 21 Accordion Fr,
  24 Nylon-str.Gt, 38 Synth Bass 1, 40 Violin, 45 PizzicatoStr, 55 OrchestraHit,
  61 Brass 1, 75 Pan Flute, 87 Bass & Lead, 90 Polysynth, 97 Soundtrack,
  109 Bagpipe, 116 Taiko.
Note: the piano preset is at bank 0 program 2 (there is no preset at program 0).
